***

One sample from the response to the [LayersController.getRasterProfile](LayersController.md#getrasterprofile)
method.

# Properties

## distanceM

> **distanceM**: `number`

How far along the line this sample was taken, in ground metres from the
line's start.

***

## value

> **value**: `null` | `number`

The band's value at this sample, or `null` where the raster holds no data or
the line runs outside the raster.
